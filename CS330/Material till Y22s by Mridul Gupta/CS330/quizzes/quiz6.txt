Q2. Consider the following pseudocode.
    int gvar = 0;
    sem_init(S, 5);  // Initialize the semaphore S with value 5
    doit(){
        sem_wait(S);
        sem_wait(S);
        gvar++;     //Increment is not atomic
        sem_post(S);
        sem_post(S);
    }
What is the minimum and maximum value of 'gvar' when 3 threads execute 'doit' concurrently to completion? (5)
